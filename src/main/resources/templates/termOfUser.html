Car Rental Terms and Conditions

1. General

By renting a vehicle from our website, you agree to comply with all the terms and conditions listed below. Please read them carefully before proceeding with your booking.

2. Eligibility

Renters must be at least 21 years old and possess a valid driver’s license with at least 2 years of driving experience. Additional age restrictions may apply for specific car categories.

3. Reservation and Payment

All reservations must be made through our official website or mobile application. A valid credit/debit card is required for payment. Full payment must be made prior to vehicle pickup.

4. Cancellation Policy

Cancellations made at least 24 hours before the scheduled pickup time will receive a full refund. Cancellations within 24 hours are subject to a cancellation fee of 20% of the total rental amount.

5. Vehicle Use

  • The vehicle must not be used for illegal activities or racing.
  • Only the registered driver(s) are permitted to operate the vehicle.
  • Smoking and transporting hazardous materials are strictly prohibited.

6. Fuel Policy

The vehicle must be returned with the same fuel level as when it was picked up. A refueling fee will be charged if the fuel level is lower upon return.

7. Damages and Liability

The renter is responsible for any damage, loss, or theft of the vehicle during the rental period. Insurance options are available to reduce liability. A report must be filed immediately in case of an accident.

8. Return Policy

Vehicles must be returned on time and at the agreed location. Late returns are subject to additional hourly or daily charges.

9. Privacy

Your personal information will be collected and stored securely for processing your rental and for legal compliance. We do not share your information with third parties without your consent.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding our terms and conditions, please contact our customer support team at support@yourcarrental.com.
